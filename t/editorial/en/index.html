I completed medical school and my ENT residency at a university medical center, and then did a fellowship in pediatric head and neck surgery abroad.

In my private clinic I see children and adults: recurrent ear infections, fluid in the ears, tonsils and polyps, sinusitis, snoring and pauses in breathing during sleep.

Every visit starts with a conversation. I explain what I found, what the options are and what the next step is, and I send a written visit summary.

Areas of care

At the clinic I diagnose and treat hearing problems, ear conditions, sinus problems, tonsil problems and breathing difficulties during sleep. Visits are scheduled at a time that suits you, with time for your questions.

Clinic diagnosis and treatment

  1. 01Ears and hearing

    Recurrent ear infections, fluid in the middle ear, hearing loss and tinnitus.

  2. 02Nose and sinuses

    Nasal congestion, recurrent sinusitis, allergy and nosebleeds.

  3. 03Snoring and breathing during sleep

    Evaluation of snoring and pauses in breathing during sleep, in children and adults.

  4. 04Throat and voice

    Hoarseness, recurrent sore throats, difficulty swallowing and voice problems.

Surgery

  1. 05Tonsils and polyps

    Surgery to remove the tonsils or polyps, including preparation for surgery and follow-up care.

  2. 06Ear tubes (ventilation tubes)

    Placement of ventilation tubes in the ears for fluid or recurrent infections.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral from a doctor?

No. You can book an appointment directly. If you have a referral, a hearing test or previous summaries, please bring them.

Can I get reimbursed by my insurance?

In most cases, yes, through supplementary or private insurance. At the end of the visit you will receive a receipt and a visit summary to submit.

From what age do you see children?

From birth. Children under 18 come with a parent or guardian.

Is the clinic accessible?

Yes. The building entrance has no steps, there is an elevator and accessible parking nearby. Full details are in the accessibility statement.

How do I cancel or reschedule an appointment?

By phone or WhatsApp, up to 24 hours before the appointment.
